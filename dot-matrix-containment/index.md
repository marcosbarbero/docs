# Dot Matrix Containment

A spreading front, a handful of walls, and the edge of the screen. Fifty levels, nine languages, and a one-time unlock that removes every ad and lets it run with no connection at all.

Free to play with ads; free play needs an internet connection so those ads can load.\*

## How to play

Something is spreading across the panel, one ring per turn, by a rule that never varies. Each turn you place one wall — or pass, which is sometimes the right move. Seal the front off before it reaches the edge and the level is yours. You are racing arithmetic, not a dice roll: the front adds a ring every turn while your budget does not grow.

## Features

- [**Fifty levels**]({{ site.baseurl }}/dot-matrix-containment/features/campaign-mode/) — each one proved sealable before it ships, by replaying a winning line through the same rules the game runs.
- [**Second Chance**]({{ site.baseurl }}/dot-matrix-containment/features/second-chance/) — rewind five turns after a leak reaches the edge, once per run, on the board you were already working.
- [**Premium Unlock**]({{ site.baseurl }}/dot-matrix-containment/features/premium-unlock/) — one payment, no ads, plays offline. Not a subscription.

## Never unfair

The spread is a fixed rule applied to the board — no randomness during play, and nothing hidden. Two identical sequences of moves always produce the identical board. Every shipped level carries a winning line that is replayed and checked before release, so a level that cannot be sealed cannot reach you.

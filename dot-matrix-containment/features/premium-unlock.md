# Premium Unlock

One payment. Every ad gone, the rewind free and instant, and the whole campaign playable with no connection at all.

**It is not a subscription.** Nothing renews, nothing recurs, there is nothing to cancel. €1.99 is the reference price; your store shows the local equivalent.

It belongs to your Apple ID or Google account rather than to a device — **Restore purchase** in Settings brings it to any device signed into the same account.

With ads removed, the advertising SDK is never initialised: no consent form, no tracking prompt, and no requests to any ad exchange. The offline play is a consequence of that, not a separate feature — with nothing to load, there is nothing to be connected for.

# Quartzon Support

Email **[marcos@marcosbarbero.com](mailto:marcos@marcosbarbero.com)**. It reaches one person, and that person wrote the games.

## Before you write, the quick answers

**"It says I need an internet connection."**
The free version does — that is how the ads that pay for it are delivered. The [Premium Unlock]({{ site.baseurl }}/quartzon/features/premium-unlock/) removes both the ads and the requirement.

**"I said no to ads and now I cannot play."**
That is deliberate, not a fault. Advertising is what pays for the free version, so declining it leaves the free tier with no funding. The screen that explains this always offers a way forward: reopen the consent form and change that answer, or buy the unlock. Nothing else is taken away in the meantime.

**"A level is locked and I have not finished the game before it."**
Levels open on the *device's* total, not on any single game's. Clearing levels in any game moves that number, so the fastest route past a locked level is often a different game. The dial shows how many more you need.

**"This level looks impossible."**
Every shipped level carries a winning line that is replayed and verified before release, so one exists.

**"I paid, and it is asking me to pay again."**
It should never do that. Open **Settings → Remove ads & play offline → Restore purchase** while connected. If it still asks, write to me with the store you bought from and the approximate date, and I will look.

**"I paid, and it says it cannot reach the store."**
That is the expected message when the store cannot be checked — the app keeps working. Reconnect and it resolves itself.

**"I bought it — does that cover all five games?"**
Yes, and any game added to the handheld later. There is one purchase and it buys the machine.

**"Can I move my progress to a new phone?"**
Not yet. Progress is stored on your device. A full device backup and transfer carries it, but there is no live sync between devices at present.

**"The app is in the wrong language."**
**Settings → Language** — it lists every language in its own name, and "System" follows the device.

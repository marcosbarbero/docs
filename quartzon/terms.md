## What the app does

Quartzon is a handheld with five games on it and one campaign running through all of them. It is free to play, supported by advertising. The free version requires an internet connection so that advertising can be delivered.

## The Premium Unlock

- It is a **one-time purchase**. It is **not a subscription**: nothing renews, nothing recurs, and there is nothing to cancel.
- The price shown at the point of purchase, in your local currency, is the price you pay. €1.99 is the reference price; the store determines the local equivalent.
- It grants, permanently and for the account that bought it: removal of all advertising from the whole handheld, and play with no internet connection.
- **It covers every game on the device, including games added later.** There is no per-game purchase and no second tier.
- It is tied to your Apple ID or Google account, not to a device. **Restore purchase** in Settings brings it to any device signed into the same account.

Full detail: [Premium Unlock]({{ site.baseurl }}/quartzon/features/premium-unlock/).

## What "free" means here

There are exactly two ways to play, and the app says so on the screen that explains them: accept advertising, or buy the unlock. If advertising is declined, the free tier has no funding and the games are not playable — the screen that says so always offers a way forward, either changing that answer or purchasing. There is no third state in which the app is both free and ad-free.

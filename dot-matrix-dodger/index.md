# Dot Matrix Dodger

A road-crossing game that looks like it fell out of a 1997 pocket handheld. Fifty hand-tuned crossings, nine languages, and a one-time unlock that removes every ad and lets it run with no connection at all.

Free to play with ads; free play needs an internet connection so those ads can load.\*

## How to play

Tap to step forward, swipe to step in any direction. One input is one step — the whole game is choosing when.

## Features

- [**Campaign**]({{ site.baseurl }}/dot-matrix-dodger/features/campaign-mode/) — 50 crossings of sliding traffic — every one verified completable without losing a life before it ships.
- [**Second Chance**]({{ site.baseurl }}/dot-matrix-dodger/features/second-chance/) — A revive after a run ends, once per run.
- [**Premium Unlock**]({{ site.baseurl }}/dot-matrix-dodger/features/premium-unlock/) — The optional one-time purchase: no ads, offline play, and records that sync across your devices.

# Dot Matrix Picture

A nonogram puzzler that looks like it fell out of a 1997 pocket handheld. Fifty puzzles, nine languages, and a one-time unlock that removes every ad and lets it run with no connection at all.

Free to play with ads; free play needs an internet connection so those ads can load.\*

## How to play

The numbers beside each row and column count the filled blocks in it. Work out which cells they can be. Tap a cell to fill it, hold to rule it out — ruling out costs nothing, and it is how the picture appears.

## Features

- [**Fifty puzzles**]({{ site.baseurl }}/dot-matrix-picture/features/campaign-mode/) — five by five up to twelve by twelve, every one proved solvable by deduction alone before it ships.
- [**Second Chance**]({{ site.baseurl }}/dot-matrix-picture/features/second-chance/) — a life back after a run ends, once per run, on the board you were already working.
- [**Premium Unlock**]({{ site.baseurl }}/dot-matrix-picture/features/premium-unlock/) — one payment, no ads, plays offline. Not a subscription.

## Never a guess

Every puzzle is checked by a solver before release. It works only by deduction and never by branching, so if a board could not be finished by reasoning it does not ship. You are never asked to guess and then charged a life for guessing wrong.

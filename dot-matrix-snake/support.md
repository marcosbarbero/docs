# Dot Matrix Snake Support

Email **[marcos@marcosbarbero.com](mailto:marcos@marcosbarbero.com)**. It reaches one person, and that person wrote the game.

## Before you write, the quick answers

**"It says I need an internet connection."**
The free version does — that is how the ads that pay for it are delivered. The [Premium Unlock]({{ site.baseurl }}/dot-matrix-snake/features/premium-unlock/) removes both the ads and the requirement.

**"I paid, and it is asking me to pay again."**
It should never do that. Open **Settings → Remove ads & play offline → Restore purchase** while connected. If it still asks, write to me with the store you bought from and the approximate date, and I will look.

**"I paid, and it says it cannot reach the store."**
That is the expected message when the store cannot be checked — the game keeps working. Reconnect and it resolves itself.

**"Can I move my scores to a new phone?"**
Not yet. Scores are stored on your device. A full device backup and transfer carries them, but there is no live sync between devices at present.

**"The game is in the wrong language."**
**Settings → Language** — it lists every language in its own name, and "System" follows the device.

## Reporting a bug

Useful to include, if you can:

- Device and OS version.
- What you were doing, and what happened instead.
- Whether it happens every time.

## Refunds

Refunds go through the store, not through me — see [Terms of Use]({{ site.baseurl }}/dot-matrix-snake/terms/). Do tell me what went wrong as well, though; a refund on its own tells me nothing.

## Response time

Usually within a couple of days. This is not a company; it is one developer with a day job.

# Dot Matrix Snake

A snake game that looks like it fell out of a 1997 pocket handheld. Two modes, fifty levels, nine languages, and a one-time unlock that removes every ad and lets it run with no connection at all.

Free to play with ads; free play needs an internet connection so those ads can load.\*

## Features

- [**Classic**]({{ site.baseurl }}/dot-matrix-snake/features/classic-mode/) — an empty grid and your own best score to beat. The snake speeds up as it grows.
- [**Campaign**]({{ site.baseurl }}/dot-matrix-snake/features/campaign-mode/) — 50 levels with walls, corridors and pillar fields, every one verified solvable before it ships.
- [**Second Chance**]({{ site.baseurl }}/dot-matrix-snake/features/second-chance/) — a revive when you die close to your best, once per run.
- [**Premium Unlock**]({{ site.baseurl }}/dot-matrix-snake/features/premium-unlock/) — the optional one-time purchase: no ads, offline play, and records that sync across your devices.

## Why Dot Matrix Snake

No account, no sign-up, and no servers of ours — scores live on your device. The whole thing is one screen: an olive LCD panel in a plastic bezel, drawn the way a segmented display actually behaves.

\* The free version needs a connection because that is how the ads that pay for it are delivered. [Premium]({{ site.baseurl }}/dot-matrix-snake/features/premium-unlock/) removes that requirement permanently, along with the ads.

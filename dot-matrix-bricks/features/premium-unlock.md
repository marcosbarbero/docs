# Premium Unlock

> One purchase, once. No ads, offline play, and your records on every device you own. It is not a subscription and nothing renews.

## What it does

Premium Unlock is Dot Matrix Bricks's optional in-app purchase. The game is fully playable for free; Premium removes the advertising and the connection requirement that comes with it.

There is no sign-up and no account. The purchase is tied to your Apple ID or Google account, so it works on every device you sign into and restores with one tap.

## What you get

- **No ads.** No interstitials between games, and no rewarded video to sit through for a second chance.
- **Play with no connection at all.** On a plane, in a tunnel, anywhere. The free version needs a connection because that is how its ads are delivered; Premium removes the requirement along with them.
- **Records sync across your devices.** Your best score and campaign progress follow you between devices you own.
- **Second chances without the video.** The same one revive per run everyone gets — you simply are not asked to watch anything for it.

## What it costs

**€1.99 or the equivalent in your local currency** — the exact figure shown on the button comes from the App Store or Google Play in your region, and is the one you pay.

**This is a one-time purchase.** It is not a subscription. Nothing renews, nothing recurs, and there is nothing to cancel.

## How to buy it

1. Open **Settings → Remove ads & play offline**, or tap **Unlock Premium** on the offline screen.
2. Confirm with your Apple ID or Google account.
3. Premium takes effect immediately — ads stop and the connection requirement is lifted in the same moment.

## Restoring it

On a new device, or after reinstalling, open **Settings → Remove ads & play offline** and tap **Restore purchase**. No account needed; the store already knows.

If the store cannot be reached, the game keeps working: a previously confirmed purchase is honoured for a grace period, and past it you are shown a "can't reach store" notice with a Restore button — never a request to buy it again.

## Free vs Premium

| | Free | Premium |
|---|---|---|
| Classic and Campaign | full | full |
| All 50 levels | yes | yes |
| Nine languages | yes | yes |
| Play with no connection | no | **yes** |
| Interstitial ads | every 3rd game over, every 3rd level clear | **none** |
| Second chance | once per run, after a video | once per run, **no video** |
| Records across devices | no | **yes** |

Nothing about the *game* differs. Paying changes what it costs you to play, not the rules — the same near-miss condition and the same one revive per run apply either way.

## Known limits

- Prices come from the store in your local currency; €1.99 is the reference price.
- Restoring needs a connection once, so the store can confirm the purchase.
- Records sync between devices signed into the same Apple ID or Google account. A full device backup restores app data regardless of Premium — that is the operating system, and it happens for everyone.

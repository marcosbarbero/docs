# Dot Matrix Bricks

A brick breaker that looks like it fell out of a 1997 pocket handheld. Fifty hand-tuned levels, nine languages, and a one-time unlock that removes every ad and lets it run with no connection at all.

Free to play with ads; free play needs an internet connection so those ads can load.\*

## How to play

Two keys move the paddle: tap to nudge one step, hold to run. Keep the ball alive and clear every breakable brick.

## Features

- [**Campaign**]({{ site.baseurl }}/dot-matrix-bricks/features/campaign-mode/) — 50 levels of rows, gaps, tough bricks and unbreakable walls — every one verified clearable before it ships.
- [**Second Chance**]({{ site.baseurl }}/dot-matrix-bricks/features/second-chance/) — A revive after a run ends, once per run.
- [**Premium Unlock**]({{ site.baseurl }}/dot-matrix-bricks/features/premium-unlock/) — The optional one-time purchase: no ads, offline play, and records that sync across your devices.
